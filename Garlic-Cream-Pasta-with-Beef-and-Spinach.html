Garlic Cream Pasta
with Beef & Spinach

Penne tossed in a rich garlic cream sauce with browned ground beef, Parmesan and wilted spinach — a one-skillet weeknight comfort dinner.

Cook20 min

      Method

      1. Cook the pastaBring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta until al dente. Reserve about 1/2 cup of pasta water, then drain.
      2. Brown the beefHeat the oil or butter in a large skillet over medium-high. Add the beef and cook until browned, 5–7 minutes, seasoning lightly and draining excess grease.
      3. Add the aromaticsReduce the heat to medium. Add the garlic, onion and red pepper flakes and cook 2–3 minutes until fragrant.
      4. Make the cream basePour in the broth, scraping up any browned bits. Stir in the heavy cream and cream cheese and simmer 2–3 minutes until smooth.
      5. Cheese and spinachStir in the Parmesan until melted, then add the spinach and cook 1–2 minutes until wilted. Adjust the seasoning.
      6. Toss and serveAdd the pasta and toss to coat, loosening with reserved pasta water as needed. Garnish with extra Parmesan and fresh herbs.

      Notes & Tips

      Save the pasta water. A splash of starchy pasta water loosens the sauce and helps it cling.

      Lean but rich. 85–90% beef keeps it from getting greasy while the cream carries the flavor.

      Add-ins welcome. Sautéed mushrooms, sun-dried tomatoes or a handful of mozzarella all work well.
