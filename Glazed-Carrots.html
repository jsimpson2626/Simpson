Glazed Carrots

Carrots simmered in white wine and butter with a touch of brown sugar until glossy and glazed, finished with fresh parsley.

Prep10 min
Cook20 min
Total~30 min
Servings amounts scale automatically
6

Ingredients

    Method

    1. Prep the carrotsPeel and rinse the carrots; chop or leave whole, your choice.
    2. SimmerPlace all the ingredients (except parsley) in a large heavy skillet, cover, bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce to medium-high and cook 5 minutes.
    3. GlazeUncover and cook until most of the liquid has reduced to a glaze. Add a splash more liquid if you want them softer.
    4. FinishRemove from the skillet and garnish with fresh parsley.

    Notes & Tips

    Wine is optional. Water, orange juice or chicken broth all work in place of the white wine.

    Cook to your texture. Pull them al dente or add liquid and keep cooking for softer carrots.
