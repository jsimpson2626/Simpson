Korean
BBQ Sauce

A sweet-savory all-purpose Korean BBQ sauce built on soy, brown sugar, mirin, and sesame oil, brightened with ginger, garlic, sriracha, and scallion, then thickened with a cornstarch slurry. One batch makes about 2 cups.

      Method

      1. Build the sauceIn a saucepan, combine the soy sauce, water, brown sugar, mirin, sesame oil, rice wine vinegar, black pepper, red pepper flakes, sriracha, ginger, garlic, and scallion. Bring to a boil over high heat, about 5 to 7 minutes.
      2. Make the slurryIn a small bowl, stir together the cornstarch and water until smooth.
      3. ThickenOnce the sauce is boiling, whisk in the cornstarch slurry. Continue cooking until the sauce is thick enough to coat the back of a spoon and hold, about 6 to 8 minutes.
      4. Cool and storeLet the sauce cool completely before pouring into a clean jar (it’s also ready to use right away). Store leftovers in an airtight container for up to two weeks.

      Notes & Tips

      Great on everything. Use it as a glaze or marinade for beef, chicken, pork, or tofu, or as a finishing sauce for rice bowls and stir-fries.

      Adjust the heat. The sriracha and red pepper flakes give a mild kick — scale them up or down to taste.

      Non-alcoholic mirin works fine here if you prefer to skip the alcohol.

      Thickness: the sauce thickens further as it cools, so pull it once it coats the back of a spoon. Loosen with a splash of water if it gets too thick.

      Storage: keeps in an airtight container in the fridge for up to two weeks.
