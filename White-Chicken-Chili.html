White Chicken
Chili

A creamy, cheesy white chili built on white beans, poached chicken breast and mild green chilis, simmered until the beans are tender and finished with plenty of cheddar jack.

Cook1 hr 30 min

    Method

    1. Poach the chickenPlace the chicken in a pan and just cover with salt and water (or bouillon). Remove the chicken, bring the water to a boil, then add the chicken back and simmer 17 minutes — don’t let it boil.
    2. Rest and diceRemove the pan from the heat and let it stand 30 minutes. Remove the chicken and dice it into small pieces.
    3. Build the baseHeat the oil in a pot over medium-high heat and sauté the onion about 15 minutes. Stir in the garlic, green chilis and spices and sauté 2 minutes more.
    4. SimmerAdd the beans and broth and bring to a boil, then simmer about 1 hour, stirring occasionally, until the beans are tender.
    5. FinishAdd the chicken and cheese and reheat well until melted and creamy.
    6. ServeServe with additional cheese on top, and diced jalapeño if you like more heat.

    Notes & Tips

    Gentle simmer, not a boil. Keeping the poaching water just below a boil keeps the chicken breast tender and easy to dice.

    Let the beans get soft. A full hour of simmering breaks the beans down just enough to give the chili its creamy body.

    Reheat the cheese in. Add the cheese at the end over gentle heat so it melts smoothly without getting grainy.

    Heat to taste. One jalapeño is the usual call here; add a second if you want it spicier.
