    Method

    1. Rinse and sortRinse the beans and pick out any stones or debris. Soaking is optional and shortens the cook time, but isn't necessary.
    2. Load the Instant PotAdd the beans, water, onion, bay leaf, salt and pepper and stir. Secure the lid and set the valve to seal.
    3. Pressure cookPressure cook on High for 50 minutes (15 minutes for soaked beans).
    4. ReleaseLet the pressure release naturally for 15 minutes, then quick-release any remaining pressure and remove the lid.

    Notes & Tips

    No soak required. Dried beans cook straight through in the Instant Pot; soaking just shortens the time.

    Kick it up. Cook in chicken broth instead of water, or add garlic powder, chili powder, cumin and a pinch of cayenne.

    Make them refried. Drain (reserving the liquid) and mash with an immersion blender, adding liquid until creamy.
