Gyros

Homemade gyro meat made from deer burger (or lean ground beef or lamb) pressed flat, baked and broiled until charred, then served in pita with tzatziki, hummus and quick pickled red onion.

Prep15 min
Cook35 min
Total~50 min
Servings amounts scale automatically
5

Ingredients

Gyro Meat
    Quick Pickled Red Onion

      Method

      1. Mix the meatCombine the ground meat, Greek yogurt, olive oil, grated onion with its juices, garlic, Italian seasoning, cumin, paprika, salt and pepper until very well blended.
      2. Press flatSpread the mixture out on parchment paper (not wax paper). Lay two more pieces of parchment on top and press as flat as possible — it usually takes up two cookie-sheet sized papers.
      3. Cut and bakeRoll into roughly 2-inch rectangles, place on a baking sheet, and bake at 400°F for 30 minutes, draining the fluids halfway through.
      4. CharRemove from the parchment into strips on the baking sheet, then broil for 2–3 minutes until nicely charred.
      5. Pickle the onionThinly slice the red onion into C-shapes (a mandoline on the thinnest setting works great). Pack into a jar, add the vinegar until the jar is about one third full, then top with water. Add salt and sugar, shake to combine, and rest in the fridge 30 minutes to 1 hour.
      6. ServeServe the gyro meat in pita with tzatziki sauce, hummus, spinach, tomatoes, banana peppers and the pickled red onion.

      Notes & Tips

      Parchment, not wax. Wax paper will melt under the broiler — parchment is essential for pressing and baking the meat.

      Grate the onion. Grating the onion with its juices works the moisture and flavor right through the meat for that classic gyro texture.

      Drain halfway. Pouring off the rendered fluids partway through baking helps the strips crisp up rather than steam.

      Make the onions ahead. The pickled red onion keeps in the fridge 5–7 days, so it’s easy to prep in advance.
