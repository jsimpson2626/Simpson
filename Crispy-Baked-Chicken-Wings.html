Crispy Baked
Chicken Wings

Extra-crispy oven-baked wings using the baking powder trick, then tossed in a simple Frank’s RedHot butter sauce. Served with blue cheese and celery. Scale by pounds of wings and the rest follows.

Prep10 min
Rest8–24 hrs
Cook~45 min
Pounds of wings amounts scale automatically

    Method

    1. Coat the wingsLine a rimmed baking sheet with foil and set a wire rack inside. Pat the chicken wings very dry with paper towels. In a large bowl, toss the wings with the baking powder and salt until thoroughly and evenly coated. Arrange on the rack with a little space between each wing.
    2. Chill uncoveredPlace the baking sheet in the refrigerator and let the wings rest, uncovered, at least 8 hours and up to 24 hours. This dries the skin for maximum crisp.
    3. BakeAdjust an oven rack to the upper-middle position and preheat to 450°F (230°C). Bake the wings 20 minutes, then flip and continue cooking until crisp and golden brown, 15 to 30 minutes longer, flipping a few more times toward the end.
    4. Make the sauceMeanwhile, combine the butter and hot sauce in a small saucepan and cook over medium heat, whisking until combined.
    5. Toss and serveTransfer the wings to a large bowl, add the sauce, and toss to coat thoroughly. Serve immediately with blue cheese dressing and celery sticks.

    Notes & Tips

    Baking powder, not baking soda. A light, even coat of baking powder raises the skin’s pH so it browns and crisps in the oven. Don’t skip it and don’t swap in baking soda.

    The overnight chill matters. Resting the coated wings uncovered in the fridge dries the skin, which is the difference between crispy and rubbery.

    Salt note: amounts assume Diamond Crystal kosher salt. For table salt, use half as much by volume (or the same weight).

    Scaling: for every pound of wings use 1 tsp (5 g) baking powder, 1 tsp (5 g) kosher salt, 2 tbsp (25 g) butter, and 2 tbsp (30 ml) Frank’s RedHot — the stepper above does this for you.
