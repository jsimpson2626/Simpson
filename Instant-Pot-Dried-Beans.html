    Method

    1. RinsePick out any stones, then rinse the beans. Add them to the pot with 6 cups of water. Don't fill past the halfway line.
    2. Pressure cookLid locked, valve to Sealing, High Pressure for 25 minutes (black beans).
    3. Natural releaseLeave it for 20 minutes, then vent the rest. Taste; if a center is chalky, cook 5 more minutes at pressure.
    4. StoreCool in the liquid. Fridge 5 days, freezer 6 months.

    Notes

    Salt after cooking. Salt or acid in the pot keeps the skins tough.

    Kidney beans. Never shorten the time; undercooked red kidney beans are toxic.

    Foam. A tablespoon of oil in the water keeps chickpeas and lentils from foaming into the valve.
