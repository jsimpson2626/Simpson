Instant Pot Dried Beans

Why not a flat ratio: beans absorb about their own volume, and on top of that the pot needs enough free liquid to come to pressure and lose some to steam. That fixed overhead is a big share at 1 cup and small at 4, so the ratio runs from about 1:3½ down to about 1:2½. Time at pressure depends on the bean, not the batch; bigger batches just take longer to reach pressure. Times are middle-of-the-road for beans under a year old; old beans can need 10 more minutes.

    Method

    1. Sort and rinseSpread the beans out and pick out any stones, shriveled beans or debris, then rinse well in a colander. If you soaked them, drain and rinse, and tick the box above so the time drops.
    2. Load the potAdd the beans, 6 cups of water, the oil, and the bay leaf, onion and garlic if using. Skip the salt and anything acidic for now. Don't fill past the halfway line.
    3. Pressure cookLock the lid, valve to Sealing, High Pressure for 25 minutes (black beans, unsoaked). Expect about 12 minutes to reach pressure first.
    4. Natural releaseLeave it for 20 minutes, then vent whatever is left. A quick release on beans makes them split and foam up into the valve.
    5. Check and seasonTaste a few. Creamy all the way through means done; a chalky center means lock the lid back on for 5 more minutes at pressure. Stir in the salt now, plus a splash of vinegar or lime if you like.
    6. StoreCool in the cooking liquid. Fridge 5 days, or freeze in 1¾-cup portions (one can's worth) with some liquid for 6 months.

    Notes & Tips

    Salt after, not before. Salt and acid (tomato, vinegar, citrus) in the pot keep the skins from softening. Add them once the beans are tender, then let them sit 10 minutes to absorb.

    Oil stops the foam. The tablespoon of oil knocks down the foam that can clog the valve. Don't skip it on chickpeas or lentils.

    Firm vs. creamy. The chart gives beans that hold their shape for salads and bowls. For refried beans, soups or hummus, add 5 minutes.

    Kidney beans must be fully cooked. Raw or undercooked red kidney beans contain a toxin. The chart times handle it; never shorten them for kidneys.

    Lentils and split peas. No soak, short times, 10-minute release. Red lentils go to mush by design; use them for dal, not salads.

    Altitude. Above 3,000 ft add 5% per 1,000 ft to the time at pressure.
