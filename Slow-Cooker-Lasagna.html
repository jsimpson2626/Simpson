    Method

    1. Make the meat sauceBrown the onion in the olive oil, add the garlic, then add the beef and sausage and cook, breaking it up, until browned. Stir in the marinara and Italian seasoning, simmer about 30 minutes, and season with salt and pepper.
    2. Mix the ricottaIn a bowl, combine the ricotta, eggs, Parmesan, parsley and a little salt and pepper.
    3. Start with sauceSpread 1 cup of meat sauce over the bottom of a greased slow cooker — this keeps it from sticking.
    4. LayerAdd a layer of oven-ready noodles (cut to fit), a ladle of meat sauce, about a quarter of the ricotta mixture, then mozzarella and Parmesan. Repeat the layers three times, finishing with mozzarella and Parmesan.
    5. Cook and restCover and cook on low about 7 hours, until bubbly and the noodles are tender. Switch to warm and let it rest so it sets up before slicing.

    Notes & Tips

    Makes a big batch. As written it feeds 12–15 in a large 8-quart cooker — halve it for a 5-quart.

    Sauce on the bottom. Starting with sauce under the first noodle layer prevents sticking and burning.

    Let it rest. Resting on warm lets the lasagna set so it slices cleanly instead of falling apart.
